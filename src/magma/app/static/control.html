Mantle to magma

Two stages. First the mantle rock partly melts at the source (batch melting): that melt is the primary basalt. Then the melt rises to the crust and cools, and the crystals are taken out as they form (fractional crystallization). Watch the order of the minerals (Bowen's reaction series) and the melt change from basalt toward andesite and beyond.

1. Melting the mantle

KLB-1 at 10 kbar: 1380 C gives about 11% melt; hotter means more melt, richer in MgO.

2. Cooling the melt in the crust

The mantle rock is dry. Water stands for a wet source, as above a subduction zone; with water and NNO the melt grows magnetite early and climbs in silica. Try 0% and QFM to see the iron-rich (tholeiitic) path instead.
