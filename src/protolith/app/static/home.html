Protolith

by Dougalien Systems

A geochemical calculator in modules. Each module opens in its own windows and runs on this computer; a rock or melt from one module can be handed to the next.

Magma to Reaction: in Magma's results window, pick a step and press Send to Reaction. Its rock goes on Reaction's card as minerals, its melt as a glass of its own oxides. Save sample writes the same thing to a file that Reaction's Open sample reads later.
